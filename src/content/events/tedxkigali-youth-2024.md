TEDxKigali Youth is our edition planned and hosted with young people, in the
schools and communities they come from. The 2024 day took the theme **First
Draft** — the idea that a thought worth having is worth saying out loud before
it is finished.

## Who spoke

Every speaker was a secondary school student, and every talk was chosen by a
committee of students. The organising team, the curation and the questions from
the floor were all theirs; the adults ran the sound desk.

## Watching the talks

Recordings are published on this website under "Talks", free to watch, and
filed under this edition.
