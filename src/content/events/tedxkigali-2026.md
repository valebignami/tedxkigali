TEDxKigali 2026 gathers speakers from across Rwanda and the region for a day of
talks, performances and conversations under the theme **Rising**. It is our
third independently organised edition, and the second to be held at the Kigali
Convention Centre — a bigger room for a bigger conversation.

## What Rising means

Every speaker this year answers a version of the same question: what comes up
when the old ground is no longer solid? That question runs through three
sessions — the land beneath us, the institutions that outlast us, and the
ideas that are only just beginning. Expect talks on climate-smart farming,
architecture, civic institutions, craft, music and technology, all filmed and
published on this website within a few weeks of the event.

## What to expect from the day

Doors open at 08:00 for registration, coffee and a chance to meet the other
guests before the first talk. The programme below runs from 09:00 to roughly
16:30, with a coffee break mid-morning and a longer lunch break around
midday. Between sessions, look out for musicians and spoken-word performers
from Kigali's own stages.

Every talk is followed by a short Q&A hosted from the audience, and the full
programme — the three sessions, the speakers in each of them, and when the
breaks fall — is published below.

## Practical information

This is where the practical details for the day go: how to reach the venue,
what to bring, and anything a guest should arrange before travelling.
Latecomers are usually seated between talks rather than during one, so it is
worth arriving early.

## After the event

Recordings of every talk go up on this website a few weeks after the event,
free to watch, under the "Talks" section. If you cannot make it in person,
check back there in December.
