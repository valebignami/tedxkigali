TEDxKigali Women is our edition held alongside **TEDWomen**, TED’s programme
on the ideas and the work of women. The 2025 afternoon took the theme **In the
Room** — not who is invited, but what changes once someone is there.

## What the afternoon was

Five speakers, two short sessions and a closing conversation with the audience,
in a room small enough that everyone could take part in it. Speakers came from
research, small business, law and the arts.

## Watching the talks

Recordings of the talks are published on this website under "Talks", free to
watch, and are filed under this edition.
