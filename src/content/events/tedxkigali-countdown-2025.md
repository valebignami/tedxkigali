TEDxKigali Countdown is our edition held as part of **Countdown**, TED's global
initiative on the climate crisis. The 2025 day took the theme **Ten Years** —
the span in which the decisions being made now stop being reversible.

## What the day was

Two sessions of talks in the morning and early afternoon, then a working
afternoon: small groups, one facilitator each, and a single question — what is
one thing you can change where you work, starting Monday.

## Watching the talks

Recordings are published on this website under "Talks", free to watch, and
filed under this edition.
