TEDxKigali Kids is our edition made for younger children, and for the adults who
bring them. The 2025 morning took the theme **Why? Why? Why?** — the question
children ask three times in a row, which is usually where the interesting part
starts.

## How the morning worked

Three short talks rather than a full day, with long breaks and tables to build
and take things apart at. Nothing ran for more than fifteen minutes.

## Watching the talks

Recordings are published on this website under "Talks", free to watch, and
filed under this edition.
