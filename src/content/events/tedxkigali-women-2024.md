The first edition of TEDxKigali Women, held alongside **TEDWomen**, took the
theme **Out Loud**: the things people are expected to keep to themselves, and
what happens when they do not.

## What the afternoon was

Four speakers and a closing conversation, in a room of about eighty people.
It set the shape the 2025 edition kept.

Recordings are published on this website under "Talks", free to watch, and
filed under this edition.
